## RDM Eq 1.2.0

- **10-band equalizer**: 31 Hz, 62 Hz, 125 Hz, 250 Hz, 500 Hz, 1 kHz, 2 kHz, 4 kHz, 8 kHz and 16 kHz (was 7 bands). The sound engine now shapes a smoother 31-band curve.
- **+ and − buttons above every band** for precise 0.5 dB steps, and a slimmer, cleaner fader design.
- **Your presets come with you**: your current curve, custom presets and edited built-in presets are converted to 10 bands automatically and keep their sound. All 18 built-in presets were rebuilt for 10 bands.
- Fewer permissions: RDM Eq no longer asks to bypass battery optimisation directly (the Status screen opens the normal battery settings instead).
- Better fit on narrow screens.

If a "Harmful app blocked" warning appears on a device with Google Play services, see docs/PLAY_PROTECT.md in the repository.
