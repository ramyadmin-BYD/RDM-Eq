## RDM Eq 1.3.0

The equalizer now matches RDM Player's equalizer, while still working on audio from other apps.

- **Same layout as RDM Player**: preset chips at the top (with a "Custom" chip), the 10 faders in a rounded card, a Save button next to the on/off switch, and the extra controls under the faders.
- **Same faders**: RDM Player's slider style. Tap or drag to set a band, double-tap to reset it. The + and − buttons stay above every band.
- **RDM Player's presets with their exact values**: Flat, Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Electronic, Hip-Hop, Vocal, Acoustic, Bass Boost, Treble Boost. RDM Eq's in-car presets (Highway Driving, Night Driving, Deep Bass and others) are still there.
- **Bass and treble enhancement** now use RDM Player's curves (up to +8 dB) and show a percentage.
- **New: Loudness** (0 to +10 dB). Makes everything louder, with the limiter keeping peaks from clipping.
- **New: Volume normalization**. A gentle compressor evens out loud and quiet songs.
- **Preamp** range is now −12 to +12 dB, the same as RDM Player.
- Balance is now also on the Equalizer screen.

Your settings, custom presets and edited presets are kept. If you were using an unedited Rock, Pop, Jazz, Classical, Acoustic, Vocal or Hip-Hop preset, it moves to the new values.

Not possible for other apps' audio: Mono playback and ReplayGain (both RDM Player-only). The Sound screen explains why.
